# Paradigms

We implement the core logic of various generative modeling paradigms, listed in chronological order by their foundational paper.

1. **Score Matching (Hyvärinen)** — Hyvärinen, "Estimation of Non-Normalized Statistical Models by Score Matching", JMLR 2005.
2. **Autoencoder** — Hinton & Salakhutdinov, "Reducing the Dimensionality of Data with Neural Networks", Science 2006.
3. **Variational Autoencoder** — Kingma & Welling, "Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes", ICLR 2014.
4. **Generative Adversarial Network** — Goodfellow et al., "Generative Adversarial Nets", NeurIPS 2014.
5. **Autoregressive** — Larochelle & Murray, "The Neural Autoregressive Distribution Estimator", AISTATS 2011. van den Oord et al., "Pixel Recurrent Neural Networks", ICML 2016. van den Oord et al., "Conditional Image Generation with PixelCNN Decoders", NeurIPS 2016.
6. **Normalizing Flow (RealNVP)** — Dinh et al., "Density estimation using Real-NVP", ICLR 2017.
7. **Continuous Normalizing Flow (FFJORD)** — Grathwohl et al., "FFJORD: Free-form Continuous Dynamics for Scalable Reversible Generative Models", ICLR 2019.
8. **Score Matching (NCSN)** — Song & Ermon, "Generative Modeling by Estimating Gradients of the Data Distribution", NeurIPS 2019.
9. **Energy-Based Model** — Du & Mordatch, "Implicit Generation and Modeling with Energy-Based Models", NeurIPS 2019.
10. **Diffusion (DDPM)** — Ho et al., "Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models", NeurIPS 2020.
11. **Flow Matching** — Lipman et al., "Flow Matching for Generative Modeling", ICLR 2023. Liu et al., "Flow Straight and Fast", ICLR 2023.
12. **Consistency Training (iCT)** — Song et al., "Consistency Models", ICML 2023. Song & Dhariwal, "Improved Techniques for Training Consistency Models", ICLR 2024.
13. **Shortcut Model** — Frans et al., "One Step Diffusion via Shortcut Models", 2024.
14. **MeanFlow** — Geng et al., "Mean Flows for One-step Generative Modeling", 2025.
15. **Improved MeanFlow** — Geng et al., "Improved Mean Flows: On the Challenges of Fastforward Generative Models", 2025.
16. **Equilibrium Matching** — Wang & Du, "Equilibrium Matching", 2025.
17. **AlphaFlow** — Zhang et al., "AlphaFlow: Understanding and Improving MeanFlow Models", ICLR 2026.
18. **Drifting Model** — Deng et al., "Generative Modeling via Drifting", 2026.
